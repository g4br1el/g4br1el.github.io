Mission: NVIDIA Workshop Preparation & Recruitment

Why

Gabriel is attending an NVIDIA pre-AI-Day workshop (22 June 2026, Munich) where NVIDIA researchers present on LLM lifecycle, inference optimization, and Physical AI. The workshop is a recruitment pipeline. Gabriel wants to apply for the “Senior Deep Learning Performance Software Engineer” role (JR1980206, Germany Remote) and use this event to make a strong impression on NVIDIA engineers — through sharp questions, corridor conversations, and demonstrated depth.

Success looks like

  • Follow all three talks without terminology lag
  • Ask 1–2 questions per talk that signal systems-level depth and research maturity
  • Hold a 5-minute corridor conversation with any speaker about their topic, connecting it to Gabriel’s HPC/profiling/optimization background
  • Leave with at least one NVIDIA engineer knowing Gabriel’s name and relevance to the Performance SW Engineer role

Constraints

  • ~3 days of preparation time (fluency strength, not storage strength)
  • No access to NVIDIA internal tools or codebases — must rely on public papers, docs, blog posts
  • Web-only Claude access (no local execution)
  • Gabriel is strong in CV/HPC/profiling but NOT deep in LLM internals or Physical AI

Out of scope

  • Actually implementing TensorRT-LLM pipelines (no cluster access for this)
  • Deep RLHF/DPO math derivations — conceptual understanding sufficient
  • Robotics hardware details (GR00T embodiment beyond software architecture)
  • JAX/DGL framework mastery (relevant to the job but not to this workshop)

LLMs hallucinate — that’s a feature of the workflow, not a bug. Having the domain expertise to identify and fix incorrect claims is precisely what makes this approach effective. If you lack the background to spot errors, LLM-generated material is dangerous. If you have it, the learning velocity is unmatched.
